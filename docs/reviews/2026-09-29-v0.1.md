# Review: Keycraft v0.1 (2026-09-29)

Run with [prompts/game-review.md](../prompts/game-review.md). Two full runs were played in headless Chromium at 1280×720 by `scripts/playtest.mjs`, a bot that reads words off the screen and types them:

- a **good** run at 60 wpm with 3% typos, buying from shops
- a **slow** run at 25 wpm with 10% typos, never buying

Both started from a fresh profile, and both died in Act 3. There were no console errors.

## Verdict
Keycraft already has a satisfying core. Typing a word to strike, and pressing a physical key to install a power, both feel good right away. Everything around that core is still prototype-grade:

- **Skill barely matters.** The 60 wpm player took zero damage for 14 straight fights, then died to the Act 3 boss in 33 seconds. The 25 wpm player with 10% typos got just as far.
- **Fights are too short.** About 4 words each, so the combo system (the main skill expression) almost never gets past ×1.5.
- **Nothing is left to chase after run one.** Every unlock fell in the first run.

**The single biggest problem is the lack of a skill curve**: rising difficulty and meta goals that reward getting better. Onboarding and visual polish come right after.

## Findings

### Major
1. **Flat difficulty, then a spike.**
   - Evidence: the good run stayed at 50/50 HP from the start until the Act 3 boss, then went from 41 HP to 0 in 33 seconds. The slow run also reached Act 3, taking 13 fights compared with the good run's 14.
   - Why it matters: getting faster doesn't change the outcome, so there's no reason to improve, and the death at the end feels arbitrary.
   - Fix: scale enemy HP and attack timers to the player's measured speed. Put real pressure into Acts 1–2. Offer Oaths as opt-in difficulty. Rebalance with a sim bot that makes choices (Roadmap, Phase 3).
2. **Fights end before combo matters.**
   - Evidence: 60 words across 14 fights, about 4 words per fight. Best combo in the whole run was 36, and the ×3 and ×4 tiers never showed up.
   - Fix: fewer, longer fights with 2–4 enemies each, or enemies that return in waves. Let combo carry between fights within an act, decaying over time.
3. **Every unlock falls in the first run.**
   - Evidence: both bots unlocked Glassblower, Cryomancer and Tycoon in their first run, including the 10%-typo bot.
   - Fix: tie unlocks to real skill (for example 97% accuracy across a whole act, or clearing Act 2 without taking damage). Add a meta-progression layer (Phase 4).
4. **No onboarding.**
   - Evidence: the menu is four rules in small gray text ([menu](v0.1/good-00-menu.png)). In the first fight, the only guidance is an 11px line at the very bottom.
   - Why it matters: new players don't learn how targeting works, what the attack timer means, or that combo exists.
   - Fix: a scripted prologue that teaches each mechanic as it appears (Phase 2).
5. **You can't tell a key has two mods.**
   - Evidence: after Gold was installed on E (which already had Ember), the E's in words show only gold ([combat](v0.1/good-05-combat-mid.png)). The Ember is invisible while you type.
   - Fix: show a two-color underline or stacked pips for multi-mod letters. Add a build panel (Phase 2).
6. **Already-typed letters are nearly invisible.**
   - Evidence: in "lobby", the typed "l" is at 35% opacity on a dark background ([slow combat](v0.1/slow-03-combat-mid.png)).
   - Fix: typed letters stay readable (for example paper-colored at 60%), and the cursor letter gets the emphasis instead.
7. **Backspace drops your target.**
   - Evidence: Backspace clears all progress on the word instead of deleting one letter.
   - Why it matters: it breaks muscle memory from every typing site, and players lose their progress.
   - Fix: Backspace deletes a letter; Tab switches target; Esc opens a pause menu.
8. **The results screen doesn't fit at 720p.**
   - Evidence: the title is clipped at the top, three unlock banners stack up, and the button sits on the bottom edge ([results](v0.1/good-13-results-final.png)).
   - Fix: a two-column layout, and one combined unlock toast.

### Minor
9. **The keyboard dominates combat.** It takes about 35% of the height while the enemy cards are mostly empty space, and the word is only 30px. Fix: make the word 44–56px, and let players hide the keyboard or show hints only.
10. **Attacks come without warning.** The threat is a thin bar plus an 11px "⚔ 3" in the corner, with no wind-up before the hit. Fix: bigger damage numbers, and a wind-up flash about 1 second before the hit.
11. **Shop is cluttered.** 7 cards in 2 uneven rows. Relics (Whetstone, Interest) look exactly like mods, and reroll is mentioned only in the footer text ([shop](v0.1/good-06-shop.png)). Fix: separate rows for Mods / Relics / Services, and a reroll button.
12. **Reward choices have no context.** Mod cards don't show which keys already have mods, and there's no rarity ([reward](v0.1/good-03-reward.png)). Fix: show a mini build preview, and add rarity (Phase 3 boons).
13. **Installing a mod is a blind choice.** Nothing shows how often each letter comes up; the tip is small gray text ([install](v0.1/good-04-install.png)). Fix: show each key's share of recent letters (e.g. "E 11%") and hits per word.
14. **Typography doesn't match.** Bungee, DM Serif, JetBrains Mono and Space Grotesk are all in use, and Bungee reads "arcade", not "mythic". Fix: the Phase 1 font set.
15. **Weak enemy art.** "ʇ" (Typo Imp) looks like a broken J, and "^" (Caret Bat) is tiny. The monsters have no presence. Fix: a real enemy art direction (Phase 1).
16. **The HUD needs hovering.** The map dots are icons with no labels, and relic info only appears on hover, which you can't do while typing. Fix: label the current node, and add a build panel.
17. **No pause menu or settings.** The game pauses only when the window loses focus. There are no volume, keyboard-layout or reduced-motion settings (Phase 2).
18. **Heatmap is hard to read.** It has no legend, and "no data" keys look almost like "good" keys. Fix: a legend, plus a hatched style for keys with no data.
19. **Blackout boss is an unverified spike.** The bot can't remember hidden words, so it may be harsher than it is for humans. Needs human playtests. Consider a longer reveal (2s) and word lengths of 4–6.

## Quick wins (< 1 day each)
6 (typed-letter contrast), 7 (Backspace), 8 (results layout), 9 (word size and keyboard toggle), 10 (attack telegraph), 13 (letter share in the install screen), 18 (heatmap legend), and making the unlock conditions harder (part of 3).

## Deep changes (need design work)
1, 2 (difficulty and fight structure), 3 (meta progression), 4 (prologue), 11–12 (shop and rewards redesign with boons), 14–15 (art direction). These are Phases 1–4 in the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).

## Protect in any redesign
- Typing a word to strike an enemy, and choosing targets by first letter.
- Installing a mod by physically pressing the key.
- Modded letters glowing inside enemy words: this is the best "my build" feedback in the game.
- The combo tier-up pop, and key clicks that climb in pitch.
- The end-of-run heatmap and nemesis pair.
- The deterministic engine and the balance bot.
