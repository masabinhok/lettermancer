# Code of Conduct

Keycraft is a game about getting better at something, one keystroke at a time. We want this project to feel the same
way for everyone who works on it: welcoming to beginners and patient with mistakes.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was new once, and "how do I…?" is always a fair question.
- Give feedback that someone can act on, and take feedback the same way.
- Respect people's time, boundaries and differences. That includes background, identity, ability, age, experience
  and typing speed.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual language and imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Pressuring people who have said no, or continuing after being asked to stop.

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community space for the project. It also
applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainers privately through the email on the
maintainer's GitHub profile, or through GitHub's private reporting. Reports are kept confidential. We'll respond as
soon as we can, and within a week at most.

## What happens next

Maintainers will review what happened and may take any action they think fits, from a private word to removing
comments or banning someone from the project. We'll tell the reporter what we did where it's appropriate to share.

This code draws on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
